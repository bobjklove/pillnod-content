Pillnod: Family Pill Reminder

Pillnod is a simple pill reminder for older parents and the family who look out for them. One app, two roles: the parent gets large, clear reminders with one big Taken button; a family member sets up the schedule and sees today's check-ins.

Pillnod is a reminder and check-in tool only. It is not medical advice. It does not suggest doses or check interactions. Ask a doctor or pharmacist about any medicine.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. You choose a role and a nickname. Pillnod never asks for a real name, email, or phone number.

How do reminders work?

Add a reminder with any name you like, pick the days and times, and optionally turn on gentle repeats (every 10 minutes, up to 3 times). The parent taps Taken on the notification or in the app; Undo is available right after.

How does family pairing work?

The family member creates an invite (QR code or 6-character code plus a security key) and the parent's phone joins. Reminder names are encrypted on the phone before anything is shared, and the key only travels between your phones. In version 1.0, pairing and check-ins are kept on each phone; live sync between phones is coming in an update.

What is Family Plus?

An optional subscription for the family-member side: full check-in history, more than one parent, more than one family viewer, and a shareable summary. The parent side is always free. Prices and any free trial are shown in the app by the App Store. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Family Plus screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings > Start over on this phone, or delete the app. Everything is stored on the phone.
