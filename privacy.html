Pillnod 隐私政策

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Contact: ddxxdtt@gmail.com

English

Pillnod is a family reminder app. It does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email, or phone number.

What stays on your phone

The nickname you choose, the reminder text you type, schedules, check-ins (taken, skipped, missed), and history are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. This data is not synced to iCloud and is not written to Apple Health. Pillnod does not use HealthKit.

Family pairing and sync

When you pair two phones, Pillnod sends the following to our server, which runs on Cloudflare: a random device id, a hashed device token, a group id and each device's role (parent or family), schedule fields (frequency, times, days, start and end dates), the number of reminders left, check-in status (taken or skipped) with its times, and an encrypted copy of reminder names, nicknames and appearance notes.

The encryption key is created on your phone and passes between your phones only through the QR code or the security key you share yourself. The server cannot read reminder names. Requests that contain readable reminder names are rejected. Notifications about missed check-ins never include a medicine name.

Schedule times and check-in status are not encrypted, so the server can see them, but they are tied only to random ids, not to your name or contact details. They are used only to keep your paired phones in sync. We do not sell them, share them, or use them for ads or analytics.

To limit abuse, the server counts requests per network address. It keeps only a salted hash of the address, not the address itself, and deletes it within about an hour. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, handles the network traffic as the host of the service.

Server data stays until you delete it. In Settings, choose Delete server data to remove your family's copy. Unpairing a phone removes that phone from the group.

Purchases

Family Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Subscription status is checked on your device.

What we do not do

No ads. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing of data. Pillnod does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Start over on this phone, or delete the app to remove everything stored on that phone.

Not medical advice

Pillnod only reminds and keeps a check-in record. It does not give medical advice, suggest doses, or check interactions.

Children

Pillnod is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

简体中文

Pillnod 是家人用药打卡提醒工具，不需要注册账号，不收集真实姓名、邮箱或手机号。

你填写的称呼、提醒文字、时间、打卡记录和历史只保存在这台 iPhone 的应用私有存储里，不同步到 iCloud，也不写入“健康”。Pillnod 不使用 HealthKit。

两台手机配对后，Pillnod 会向我们的服务器（运行在 Cloudflare 上）发送：随机设备编号、设备令牌的哈希、群组编号和每台设备的角色（父母或家人）、提醒规则（频率、时间、日期、起止日期）、剩余提醒次数、打卡状态（已服用或已跳过）及其时间，以及加密后的提醒名称、称呼和外观备注。

密钥在你的手机上生成，只通过二维码或你自己告知的安全密钥在手机之间传递，服务器读不懂提醒名称；带有明文提醒名称的请求会被拒绝；漏服提醒推送不会包含药品名称。

提醒时间和打卡状态没有加密，服务器可以看到，但它们只关联随机编号，不关联你的姓名或联系方式，只用于让配对的手机保持同步。我们不出售、不共享，也不用于广告或统计。

为防止滥用，服务器会按网络地址统计请求次数，只保存加盐哈希而不是地址本身，并在约一小时内删除。服务的托管方 Cloudflare 会作为托管服务商处理网络流量。

服务器上的数据会保留到你删除为止。在“设置”里选择“删除服务器数据”即可清除你们家的副本；取消配对会把这台手机移出群组。

家人 Plus 订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息。

没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪，不出售或分享数据，不跨 App 或网站追踪你。

在“设置 → 在这台手机上重新开始”或删除应用，即可清除这台手机上的全部数据。

Pillnod 只做提醒和打卡记录，不提供医疗建议，不计算剂量，也不检查药物相互作用。
